# INSIGHTS.md

## Insight 1: High Player Traffic Concentrated in Specific Zones

### What caught our eye
Player movement is not evenly distributed across the map. Instead, there are clear “high-traffic corridors” where most players converge.

### Evidence
- Dense clustering of position paths in certain regions
- Multiple player paths overlapping repeatedly in the same areas
- Sparse or near-empty regions elsewhere on the map

### Actionable Interpretation
- These zones likely represent:
  - High-value loot areas
  - Key traversal routes
- Designers can:
  - Redistribute loot to underused regions
  - Introduce incentives (objectives, rewards) in low-traffic areas

### Impact on Metrics
- Improves map utilization
- Reduces predictability of player movement
- Encourages exploration

### Why a Level Designer should care
If players consistently ignore large portions of the map, those areas are effectively wasted design effort. Balancing traffic improves gameplay variety and engagement.

## Insight 2: Combat Events Cluster Around Movement Intersections

### What caught our eye
Kills and deaths tend to occur at intersections of major movement paths rather than being randomly distributed.

### Evidence
- Kill and death markers are concentrated in areas where multiple player paths cross
- Few combat events in isolated or low-traffic regions

### Actionable Interpretation
- These areas naturally become “hot zones”
- Designers can:
  - Intentionally reinforce these zones with cover and verticality
  - Or reduce congestion by offering alternative routes

### Impact on Metrics
- Affects combat frequency and pacing
- Influences player survival rates
- Shapes player engagement intensity

### Why a Level Designer should care
Understanding where fights happen helps in designing balanced encounters. Over-concentration may lead to frustration, while under-concentration may lead to boring gameplay.

## Insight 3: Bots Follow Simpler and More Predictable Paths

### What caught our eye
Bot movement patterns are noticeably more linear and less varied compared to human players.

### Evidence
- Bot paths show:
  - Fewer direction changes
  - More uniform trajectories
- Human players show:
  - Erratic movement
  - Frequent path deviations
  - Exploration behavior

### Actionable Interpretation
- Bot behavior may feel predictable to experienced players
- Designers can:
  - Introduce randomness in bot navigation
  - Simulate human-like decision-making (looting, retreating, flanking)

### Impact on Metrics
- Improves perceived AI quality
- Increases challenge and engagement
- Reduces player boredom against bots

### Why a Level Designer should care
If bots are too predictable, they reduce the overall challenge and immersion of the game. Improving bot behavior directly enhances gameplay quality.

---

## Summary

These insights demonstrate that:

- Player behavior is strongly influenced by map design
- Movement patterns directly impact combat distribution
- AI behavior plays a critical role in gameplay experience

The visualization tool makes these patterns immediately visible, enabling faster and more informed design decisions.
